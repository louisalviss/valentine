Observation sheet 01

What the operator keeps an eye on.

Performance

Weight, render, cache.

Find slow templates, heavy assets, caching gaps and page-level bottlenecks before they become chronic problems.

Technical SEO

Crawl, index, structure.

Surface metadata, internal linking and structural issues as a prioritized plan instead of a raw error dump.

Content

Freshness, position, gaps.

Flag stale pages and exact sections that need review rather than rewriting everything.

Maintenance

Compatibility, rollback, risk.

Review dependencies and recovery path before a plugin or code change is approved.
