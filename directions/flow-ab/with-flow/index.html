Editorial statement / 001

Not another dashboard. A maintenance discipline.

SiteOps AI audits the real site first. It does not begin by generating changes.

It ranks issues by impact, risk and effort so the queue reflects what matters rather than what is easiest to detect.

Material changes remain reviewable before execution. Approved work is checked after it reaches the live site.
