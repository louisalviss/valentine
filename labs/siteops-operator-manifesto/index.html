02 / CoverageOne review queue, four disciplines

What deserves
a closer look?

Start with what is actually happening on the site. Group the issues by discipline before deciding what to do next.

01

Find the weight.

Slow templates, heavy assets, caching gaps and page-level bottlenecks.

02

Check the structure.

Crawlability, indexing, metadata, internal links and technical SEO.

03

Spot content drift.

Stale pages, positioning gaps and broken links.

04

Review maintenance risk.

Plugin compatibility and maintenance changes that need a review first.
