One road.
Five worlds.
One stylized ride.

Everything shifts to a unified low‑poly language: car, road, landmarks, weather and light. The journey moves through ocean, forest, desert, city and summit — each with its own road design and camera rhythm.

01 / mist forest

Dense pines.
Tight wet road.

A narrow forest lane with close trees, fog layers and light shafts. The camera tucks in and feels faster.

02 / desert pass

Open sweep.
Heat and distance.

The road broadens into longer arcs with mesas, dunes, arches and dust. The camera glides wider and lower.

03 / neon city

Reflective blacktop.
Vertical light.

Multi-lane elevated roadway, towers, signs, rain and vivid neon. The camera lifts to reveal the urban system.

04 / aurora summit

Cliff road.
Crystal sky.

A narrow alpine pass, snow, crystals and aurora. The last stretch opens into a final, quieter cinematic pullback.
